# KEYFORGE — product context

**What it is:** an online store for mechanical keyboards, keycaps and switches, aimed at enthusiasts, gamers, programmers, creators and everyday typists in the Philippines (prices in PHP; GCash, Maya, COD).

**Mode:** Persuade on the storefront (home, product, builder); Operate on checkout, account and admin.

**Brand:** premium technology / enthusiast culture. Dark, glassy, with a themeable RGB accent (cyan, purple, blue, red, green, orange). The interactive keyboard is the hero artifact.

**Principles:** premium, interactive, fast, professional, easy to use. Motion is short (200–600 ms), intentional, and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Constraints:** no build step; plain ES modules; sample data only (no real payments); WCAG-minded contrast, focus states and keyboard access.
